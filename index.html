    Engineering essentials checklist

    Grow steadily across academics, practical skills and communication.

    🎯 Placement journey

    1. Build foundationsProgramming or branch fundamentals and communication.
    2. Practise problem solvingFor software roles, learn arrays, strings, searching, sorting and data structures gradually.
    3. Understand your target roleRead job descriptions and note skills, eligibility, location and deadlines.
    4. Build proof of skillsProjects, lab work, internships or team contributions you can explain honestly.
    5. Prepare your profileMake a clear resume and portfolio/GitHub where relevant.
    6. Practise interviewsTechnical fundamentals, project explanations and behavioural questions.
    7. Apply safelyUse official career pages. Never pay for a guaranteed job offer.
    Hiring criteria change by role and hiring cycle. The current official vacancy is the source of truth.
